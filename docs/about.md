# About Polarism

Polarism grew from research on exciton-polariton condensates at the Faculty of Physics, University of Warsaw. The project was created as part of an NCN OPUS grant, with support from the [Polariton Exciton Research Group](https://polariton.fuw.edu.pl/) and [ICM](https://icm.edu.pl/) computing infrastructure.
